# Working Language

## Career record

The complete, factual body of a person's professional experience.

## Evidence

A specific activity, achievement, metric, project, decision, or outcome that supports a professional claim.

## Opportunity

A captured job before or during the decision to pursue it.

## Assessment

An honest view of fit, gaps, risk, trajectory, and compensation for an opportunity.

## Application document

A CV or cover letter made for a particular opportunity.

## Source material

Raw material that supports the career record, such as rough notes, previous CVs, imported documents, or project information.

## Provenance

The context needed to understand where a fact, extraction, estimate, or suggestion came from.
