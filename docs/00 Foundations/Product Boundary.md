# Product Boundary

currnt is a career-application workspace for people with substantial professional history. It helps them keep the full record of that history, decide whether a role is worth pursuing, and produce thoughtful application documents.

It is not intended to become a full job-search platform. It should not try to replace job boards, automate applications, or create an always-on job-discovery machine.

## What currnt is for

- Maintaining a rich, durable [[Working Language#Career record|career record]].
- Capturing and understanding opportunities found elsewhere.
- Making informed pursue-or-pass decisions.
- Creating truthful CVs and cover letters with strong evidence behind them.

## What currnt is not for

- Replacing job boards or professional networks.
- Applying to jobs on the user's behalf.
- Treating job searching as a high-volume productivity game.
- Making unsupported career claims or decisions without user review.
