## Product loop

1. Build and maintain a rich [[01 Product Areas/Career Profile|Career Profile]].
2. Bring in an [[00 Foundations/Working Language#Opportunity|opportunity]] from wherever it was found.
3. Understand it through [[01 Product Areas/Job Tracking and Assessment|assessment]].
4. Decide whether to pursue it.
5. Shape truthful, relevant [[01 Product Areas/Application Documents|application documents]].
6. Keep the source, decision, assessment, and documents connected.

## Vault map

- `00 Foundations` holds the product's intent, language, and experience principles.
- `01 Product Areas` holds the main user-facing verticals.
- `02 Shared Capabilities` holds product capabilities that serve more than one area.
- `03 Decisions` is reserved for decisions once they are made.
- `99 Inbox` is for unprocessed thoughts and observations.
